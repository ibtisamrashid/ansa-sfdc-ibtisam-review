# Sequence Hub

The customer sequence library and its sequence details.

## Language

**Sequence Hub**:
The customer library of saved DNA sequences and their order history.

**Sequence details**:
The view of one saved sequence, including its DNA, metadata, annotations, and available actions.

**Manual annotations**:
Named regions of a sequence identified by a user.

**Plannotate annotations**:
Sequence features identified automatically by Plannotate.
